#4471 is marked known: one issue, one fix queued. It is not one issue. Its reports have more than one cause. The queued parser fix closes only one of them.

Fixing the parser will not fix the booking-v3 form or the one-day moves. Keep their tickets open until each has its own fix.

Counted once per report id: the export pages overlap, so some reports come twice.

What each one is

  • parser: the shared parser reads every date month first. Our tenants write the day first, so day and month swap: 7 April becomes 4 July. Every tenant, through the app, email and the booking-v2 form.
  • hint: the booking-v3 form prints MM/DD/YYYY under its date field, then reads the date day first, the tenant’s way. People type what the hint says, and day and month swap. Same symptom as parser, different code, different fix: only the form of booking-v3 tenants.
  • shift: the date moves by one day, or one meeting shows on different days for people in different places. No swap explains it. It points at a time-zone conversion, not at the parser. The desk read these as a one-day move (desk-notes.json), but they were counted under parser.

How the split was made

By the rules in src/lib/kb/causes.ts: the tenant’s locale and form decide the cause, not the intake tag. Intake guesses from the reporter’s browser, and on #4471 it was wrong both ways. Merging by subject line (decision 0001) put every cause into one ticket, because the reports all say the same thing: wrong day.

Lessons

  • The same complaint is not the same bug. Before a big ticket is marked known, sort it by cause, not by subject.
  • A report the cause rules cannot place needs a person to read it, and its own count.
